⚡ Full-Duplex Conversational Voice Pipeline (Sub-500ms Turnaround)

Real-time audio negotiation, low-latency streaming inference, and natural conversational barge-in mechanics.

Sub-Second Verified
Stage 01 🎙️
Audio Streaming Ingestion
Inbound caller audio arrives over Asterisk WebRTC (WSS) or Exotel WebSocket in 20ms frames (160 samples, 8kHz mono Linear PCM / G.711 PCMA).
⚡ Frame Size: 20ms Paced Chunking
Stage 02 🗣️
Streaming Speech-to-Text
Sarvam AI saaras:v3 or Deepgram Nova-2 processes streaming PCM audio frames with persistent TLS sockets and adaptive endpointing silence thresholds.
⚡ Latency: ~210ms Streaming STT
Stage 03 🧠
In-Memory Cosine Vector RAG
Tenant-isolated knowledge vectors pre-warmed in local RAM via sentence-transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) retrieve exact verified facts without database network latency.
🔍 Retrieval: <18ms (Zero Hallucination)
Stage 04 🚀
Groq LLM Reasoning & TTFT
Groq LPUs process Llama-3-70B/8B prompts with strict system boundaries, conversational fillers, and Indian colloquial intent detectors.
🚀 TTFT: ~165ms Time-to-First-Token
⚡
Instant Conversational Barge-In: When caller speech is detected while the AI is speaking, the pipeline sends a clear event to the audio track queue within <40ms. The ongoing speech queue is drained instantly, ensuring a natural, human-like interruption experience.

📞 Human Escalation & Supervisor Call Transfer Flow

When a caller asks to speak to a human or has a complex dispute:

  1. The AI agent acknowledges the request politely ("Connecting you to our senior counselor now, please hold on...").
  2. The audio queue completes playback so the farewell announcement is clearly heard.
  3. A standard RFC 3515 SIP REFER message is transmitted to Asterisk PBX with the supervisor phone number (e.g., 07022721098).
  4. Asterisk bridges the caller's mobile directly to the human counselor's phone, releasing our AI audio track.
